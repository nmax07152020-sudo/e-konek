Submission Guide
Workflow for BHW users
① Enter the patient encounter details.

② Select the heat-related condition.

③ Submit. The system assigns the reporting barangay automatically when logged in as BHW.

④ Admin can review the report and monitor the area-wide dashboard.
🔒 Access rules

BHW: own barangay only.
Admin: all barangays.
Public: aggregate statistics only.
Offline: save locally, then auto-sync when online.
Announcements: Admin and BHW can publish; both appear on Public Dashboard. Admin announcements also pop up for relevant BHW accounts.
Inventory: Admin manages stocks; BHW can view assigned barangay stock.

System Guide
Super El Niño heat-related illness reporting flow.
🌐 Public
Open to everyone

Shows aggregate totals, trends, conditions, and cases per barangay. No patient names or private details.

🛡️ Admin
Area / city health admin

Views all reports, all barangays, review status, critical cases, and exports.

🩺 BHW
Barangay-restricted

Sees only the assigned barangay, its patient records, illness totals, and can submit reports for that barangay.

📡 Unit Monitoring
Admin-only

Shows all 16 barangay units as Online, Pending, or Offline based on their latest heartbeat.

🛡️ Live security: login, role restrictions, barangay locking, patient permissions, and account management are enforced server-side by Google Apps Script.
